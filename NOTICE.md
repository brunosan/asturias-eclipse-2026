## Sources and services

The analysis and application cite or use:

- [Instituto Geográfico Nacional (IGN)](https://astronomia.ign.es/) for public
  eclipse timing, eclipse geometry, visibility data, and terrain services;
- [IDEE elevation-profile service](https://api-processes.idee.es/processes/elevationProfile)
  for MDT05 terrain profiles;
- [Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)](https://www.ine.es/) for municipal
  population context;
- [Eurostat GISCO](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/gisco) for regional
  boundary geometry;
- [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) and
  the [OSRM project](https://project-osrm.org/) for free-flow route geometry;
- [AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/) for official weather guidance and cloud
  climatology context;
- [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) for historical and live model access;
- [DGT](https://www.dgt.es/) for official traffic information and DATEX II
  incident data;
- the Principado de Asturias, 112 Asturias, FICYT, Castilla y León, ESA, and
  the cited local authorities for access, mobility, safety, and event
  information.

Astronomy calculations use
[Astronomy Engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy), installed as a
package dependency under its own licence. Browser rendering uses
[Playwright](https://playwright.dev/), also under its own licence.

Raw third-party mirrors and source screenshots are not redistributed here.
The derived evidence retains source names, URLs, retrieval dates, and caveats;
see [data/SOURCES.md](data/SOURCES.md) and
[docs/METHODS-AND-SOURCES.md](docs/METHODS-AND-SOURCES.md).
